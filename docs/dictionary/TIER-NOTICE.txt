Tiered runtime adaptation, v2

Default bundled pack: 日常通识 / Everyday & General (daily).
Optional downloads: 进阶阅读 / Extended Reading (extended),
专业参考 / Specialist Reference (specialist).

Additional modifications: source sense selection into disjoint tiers using
fixed frequency signals and explicit general-familiarity vocabulary; bounded
per-record zlib JSON bodies; shared numeric identities, surfaces and origin
metadata. The full original audit evidence remains a separately hashed developer
artifact. Tiering does not rewrite definitions, examples or ancestor context.

Frequency selection signals: wordfreq 3.1.1, Copyright 2022 Robyn Speer.
Frequency data is available under CC BY-SA 4.0 with constituent attributions.
Credit also Marc Brysbaert et al., authors of the freely available SUBTLEX data,
and the other sources listed in the accompanying WORDFREQ-NOTICE.md, retained
in full. No dictionary definitions/examples are imported from wordfreq. Its
software license is Apache 2.0; this distribution does not bundle that library's
code. The independent preparation script only reads its documented data format.

Phrase-attestation signals are aggregate counts/years from the pinned raw
Wiktionary extraction. No external quotation/reference text is redistributed.
Ordinary example admission still requires positive source-template evidence;
counts used for coverage selection do not constitute rights clearance.

LexiCue modifications: English-only selection and structured SQLite conversion;
omission of proper-name-only records except abbreviation/relationship targets;
removal of translations, etymologies, media, quotations and semantic graphs;
preservation of original gloss ancestry, labels and pronunciations; positive
ordinary-example template verification against the same dump's source pages;
resolution of explicit form/alternative/MediaWiki redirects into verified
substantive targets; addition of lookup indexes and source/path identifiers.
See POLICY.md, build-audit.json and the versioned source lock for exact rules.

No Chinese definition layer, Wordnet, WikDict translation layer, Longman,
Collins, audio, images, or external quotations are bundled. Ordinary example
selection relies on contributor licensing and positive source-template evidence;
it is not a claim of individual authorship clearance. See POLICY.md limitations.
