PhraseDict data attribution
===========================

This resource combines the original LexiCue phrase list with filtered data
from Kaikki's machine-readable English Wiktionary data:

https://kaikki.org/dictionary/English/pos-phrase/

The Wiktionary-derived data is available under the Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International license (CC BY-SA 4.0). The
underlying Wiktionary content is available at https://www.wiktionary.org/.

The original LexiCue phrase entries are retained at the beginning of the
resource and are not part of the Wiktionary-derived data.
